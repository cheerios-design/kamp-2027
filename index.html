Seven home cities

Who travels farther?

Sakarya is closer for Istanbul, Ankara, Bolu and Adana. Bursa is closer for Izmir, Isparta and Antalya. Drive times are rough one-way estimates by road. Count who comes from each city before voting.

Option A vs Option B

Bigger mountains, or shorter trips?

Bursa has real mountain hiking on Uludağ and the only site we found that lists a meeting hall. Sakarya is closer for most of the group, has more places to stay, and sits next to the Bolu rental contacts.

A · Bursa

Pros

  • One-stop site: Nefes Dağyenice lists bungalows, glamping tents, a restaurant and a meeting hall, about 20 min from Bursa center.
  • Real mountain hiking: Uludağ, the highest peak in the Marmara region, with lakes near 2,200 m.
  • New Testament tie-in: İznik is ancient Nicaea, home of the Council of 325.
  • Closer for Izmir, Isparta and Antalya.
  • City services: hospitals, shops and an airport nearby.

Cons

  • 3–5 hours from Istanbul, Ankara and Bolu.
  • Uludağ in May–June can still have snow, ice, wind and heavy weekend traffic.
  • İznik sites are about 1 hour from Bursa and list no meeting room.
  • Bolu gear travels 3.5–4 hours.

B · Sakarya

Pros

  • Close to most people: about 2 h from Istanbul, 3 h from Ankara, 1.5–2 h from Bolu.
  • Many places to stay around Sapanca, so there's room to compare and bargain for a group price.
  • Easy gear logistics: the Bolu rental companies are close.
  • Moderate hikes: Soğucak plateau at 1,100 m, waterfalls, lakeshore paths.
  • Milder weather for outdoor lessons in late May and June.

Cons

  • Lower forest hiking with no big summit day.
  • Meeting rooms unconfirmed: most sites aim at couples and families.
  • Weekend crowds and prices: the İl Ormanı bungalows were 3,500 TL weekdays, 4,500 TL weekends in 2025.
  • 6–8 hours from Izmir, Isparta and Antalya.

Do the trails start at the establishment?

None start right at a bungalow door. The closest is the lakeshore walk, which starts at the gate of Sakarya İl Ormanı park, the same park as its bungalows. Every other trailhead needs a drive: 6 minutes in İznik, 10–20 minutes around Sapanca, and about an hour from Nefes Dağyenice to Uludağ. The dashed lines on the maps show these drives.
